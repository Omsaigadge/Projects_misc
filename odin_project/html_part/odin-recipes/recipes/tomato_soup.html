Tomato Soup


Description

Tomato soup is a timeless and comforting dish that has earned a place on tables worldwide for its simplicity and rich flavor. Made primarily from ripe tomatoes, the soup is often blended with aromatic ingredients like garlic, onion, and basil to create a harmonious balance of sweetness and tanginess. A splash of cream or milk is frequently added to enhance its velvety texture, while spices such as black pepper or paprika provide subtle warmth. Whether served as a starter or a light main course, tomato soup is often paired with crusty bread or a classic grilled cheese sandwich, making it a versatile and satisfying choice for any meal.

What sets tomato soup apart is its adaptability to different tastes and dietary preferences. It can be prepared with fresh tomatoes for a vibrant, garden-fresh flavor or canned tomatoes for convenience and consistency. Variations abound, from chunky styles with vegetable pieces to smooth, pureed versions. Additionally, tomato soup is a nutritional powerhouse, offering a good source of vitamins A and C, as well as antioxidants like lycopene, which supports overall health. Loved by people of all ages, tomato soup is a go-to option for cozy evenings, rainy days, or whenever a bowl of wholesome comfort is needed.

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes – 6-8 medium-sized, ripe, chopped
  • Onion – 1 medium, finely chopped
  • Garlic – 2-3 cloves, minced
  • Carrot – 1 small, peeled and chopped (optional, for added sweetness)
  • Vegetable or Chicken Stock – 3 cups
  • Butter or Olive Oil – 1 tablespoon
  • Salt – To taste
  • Black Pepper – To taste
  • Fresh Basil – A few leaves, chopped (optional, for garnish)
  • Cream or Milk – ¼ cup (optional, for creaminess)
  • Sugar – 1 teaspoon (optional, to balance acidity)
  • Croutons – For topping (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the Ingredients
    • Wash and chop the tomatoes, onion, carrot (if using), and garlic.
  2. Heat the Butter or Oil
    • In a large pot or saucepan, heat butter or olive oil over medium heat.
  3. Sauté the Aromatics
    • Add the chopped onion and garlic to the pot and sauté until fragrant and translucent.
  4. Add Tomatoes and Carrots
    • Stir in the chopped tomatoes and carrots (if using). Cook for 5-7 minutes until softened.
  5. Add Stock
    • Pour in the vegetable or chicken stock. Stir well and bring to a gentle boil.
  6. Simmer
    • Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let the soup simmer for 15-20 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
  7. Blend the Soup
    • Use an immersion blender or transfer the soup to a blender in batches to puree until smooth.
  8. Season
    • Return the soup to the pot (if blended separately) and season with salt, black pepper, and sugar (if needed).
    • Stir in cream or milk (if using) for a creamy texture.
  9. Garnish and Serve
    • Ladle the hot soup into bowls, garnish with fresh basil leaves and croutons (if desired), and serve with crusty bread or a grilled cheese sandwich.
